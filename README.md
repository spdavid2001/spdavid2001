### Hi, I'm David 👋

I work where **credit, markets and AI systems** meet. Most of what I build is private. This page describes the themes it covers.

---

**🏦 Credit & markets research**
- *Market intelligence pipeline:* free public data turned into dated facts, then testable theses, then a monthly narrative with positioning implications. You can query it in plain English, and every number traces back to a source.
- *Onchain credit research:* a reproducible study of who holds allocation authority in onchain lending, what actually constrains them, and what an outside allocator can independently verify.

**🧠 Evidence & reasoning**
- *Claim investigation:* independent models analyze a claim blind and commit their work before seeing each other's, then cross-examine. Evidence is checked against its sources and explanations become probabilistic predictions that get scored as they resolve.

**🤖 Agentic engineering**
- *Multi-agent workflows:* coding agents from different model families build, validate and review work in isolated sandboxes, with a human approving every merge.
- *Agent observability:* a playful real-time view of which agents are working on what.

**📈 Personal decision tools**
- Career-opportunity screening that keeps "fits my resume" separate from "fits my goals."
- Training analytics for marathon prep.
